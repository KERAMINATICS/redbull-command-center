# Red Bull-Inspired Command Center Playbook

This is an independent portfolio prototype inspired by publicly visible Red Bull brand patterns. It is not an official Red Bull product, is not endorsed by Red Bull, and does not use private Red Bull data.

## Public brand cues translated into the interface

Red Bull’s public digital ecosystem combines energy drinks, athletes, events, gaming, dance, stories, live broadcasts, recaps, and Red Bull TV. Its public company language includes “Giving Wiiings to People and Ideas,” while Red Bull Media House describes a mission around “beyond the ordinary” stories across sport, culture, and lifestyle.

The interface therefore uses a live-event/control-tower structure, concise kinetic copy, warm white editorial space, deep navy control surfaces, red action signals, and restrained yellow energy accents. It uses original abstract assets rather than protected logos or campaign artwork.

## Evidence status rules

`PUBLIC CONTEXT` refers to information observed on official public Red Bull pages. `PUBLIC PROXY` refers to external market or category information that is directionally relevant but not Red Bull India performance. `SIMULATED MODEL` refers to synthetic portfolio data. `DECISION RULE` refers to a proposed operating rule that must be validated with real data and stakeholders.

## Voice rules

Use direct, active, high-energy language without sounding like a generic SaaS product. Prefer “Find the next move,” “Trace the signal,” “Measure the lift,” “Watch the replay,” and “Scale only when the evidence clears the bar.” Avoid claims that imply official Red Bull access, endorsement, or observed India performance.

## Analytical rules

Every recommendation must include the decision owner, the modeled value at stake, the evidence status, the uncertainty, the pilot design, and a stop/continue/scale rule. Charts must clarify a commercial decision and use precise units. Simulated data must remain visibly labeled in the dashboard and portfolio narrative.
