# What Would Change My Recommendation?

The current recommendation is to renew the strongest quality-adjusted inventory, renegotiate proof-poor reach, and test retail and digital activation before scaling. The recommendation is intentionally conditional.

| If this evidence arrives | What it would mean | Recommendation change |
|---|---|---|
| Rights fee rises more than 35% with no new measurement rights | Fee inflation exceeds modeled value creation. | Move the affected asset from renew to renegotiate or exit. |
| Control-market lift is below 15% after contribution margin and activation cost | Commercial impact does not clear the working hurdle. | Stop incremental funding and redesign the activation. |
| Pre-treatment trends fail for the proposed control markets | The counterfactual is not credible. | Do not claim incrementality; move to a measurement test. |
| Consent and CRM join rate is below 70% | Direct conversion and clean-room matching are too incomplete. | Treat the asset as exposure-only until instrumentation improves. |
| Retail uplift disappears after stockout and seasonality controls | The apparent sales response is not incremental. | Remove retail value from the renewal case. |
| Lower-bound ROI interval is negative | The upside depends on a fragile assumption. | Cap investment at test budget and negotiate performance clauses. |
| Brand-lift evidence improves while direct conversion remains weak | The asset may create long-term brand value not visible in short-term sales. | Renew only with a brand-lift and long-term measurement plan. |
| A simple baseline beats the advanced model out of time | Complexity is not adding decision quality. | Use the simpler model until data volume improves. |

## The decision rule

**Renew or scale only when commercial value remains positive after rights fee, the causal design is credible, and the lower-bound evidence is not negative.** If any one of these conditions fails, the default is renegotiation or a bounded test rather than a blanket renewal.

## Why this matters

A sponsorship dashboard becomes commercially useful when it can change its mind. The point of the model is not to prove that every asset worked. The point is to expose the assumptions that management can challenge before committing the next season's budget.
