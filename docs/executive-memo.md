# IPL Sponsorship Commercial Value Engine
## Executive decision memo

### Decision

**Do not approve a blanket renewal or scale-up from this portfolio simulation. Renegotiate measurement access and performance clauses, then run a bounded treatment/control pilot before making the renewal decision.**

The synthetic control-lift contribution proxy totals **₹3.67m** against **₹71.7m** of modeled rights fees. That is **5.1% fee coverage** and **−94.9% modeled ROI** under the stated assumptions. This is not evidence that any real sponsorship asset loses money: all sponsor-level inputs are synthetic and the public reach figures do not reveal sponsor-level incremental profit.

The model separately estimates **₹641.9m of media-equivalent value**. That is an exposure-based proxy, not cash contribution or incremental profit, and it is not added to the control-lift contribution used for ROI.

### What to do next

| Decision lane | Operating recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Jersey front, Boundary LED, Strategic timeout, Digital match hub, Fan park | **Test / measure first** | These assets fall in the model’s medium/high exposure-quality bands. A quality score prioritizes measurement; it is not proof of commercial return. |
| Creator matchday, Retail take-over, Training ground | **Renegotiate / hold pending evidence** | The simulated quality signal is lower and the outcome link needs stronger instrumentation. Do not infer that the asset should be exited from synthetic values. |
| Portfolio renewal | **No scale authorization from this model** | No asset clears its modeled rights fee on the control-lift contribution proxy, and there are no observed treatment/control outcomes. |

The Jersey front is the first *measurement candidate* by quality score. Its simulated control-lift contribution proxy is **₹0.89m** versus a simulated rights fee of **₹18.4m**. This is a pilot prioritization signal only—not a renewal approval.

### Analytical approach

The dashboard scores exposure quality using visibility duration, placement, audience, engagement, and conversion-quality inputs. It then displays direct response, assisted-plus-retail response, and a conservative control-market lift proxy. The primary commercial ROI calculation uses the control-lift contribution proxy only. Media-equivalent value is kept separate to prevent impressions or reach from being treated as profit.

The model’s deterministic perturbation range around synthetic control-lift inputs is **−95.3% at P10, −94.9% at P50, and −94.5% at P90**. It is a modeled scenario range, **not a real-world confidence interval** and not a causal estimate. The underlying fields are not randomized or observed sponsor outcomes.

### Commercial negotiation position

Before the next rights decision, seek:

1. An asset-level exposure log with stable asset IDs, placement, duration, legibility, audience source, and frequency.
2. Consented, de-identified outcome linkage through a clean room or equivalent workflow.
3. Market-level sell-out, margin, stockout, and activation cost data at a joinable grain.
4. A pre-registered treatment/control design with pre-trend checks, outcome window, exclusions, and a stop rule.
5. A performance tranche or fee concession tied to data availability and measured incremental contribution.

The simulator’s **50% ROI scale bar, 15% renegotiation bar, and 0% test boundary** are proposed decision rules for discussion, not market benchmarks. Scaling in a real decision should require a measured contribution result and a lower-bound uncertainty interval above zero.

### What management should approve now

Approve the data contract, instrumented pilot, and measurement/fee clauses—not incremental spend. Replace all synthetic exposure and funnel assumptions with verified logs before using the dashboard for a real renewal. Public reach is context, not proof of sponsor-level incrementality.
