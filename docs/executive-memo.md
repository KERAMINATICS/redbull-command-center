# IPL Sponsorship Commercial Value Engine
## Executive decision memo

### Decision

**Renew the high-quality asset pool, renegotiate proof-poor reach, and fund a controlled measurement sprint before scaling the next rights package.** The modeled portfolio produces **₹119.2m of base commercial value against ₹71.7m of rights fees**, equivalent to **66.3% modeled ROI**. The result is decision-useful only as a structured scenario because asset-level conversion and retail fields are simulated.

### Why this is the right commercial call

The portfolio should not be renewed as one undifferentiated bundle. The model separates media-equivalent exposure from business response and ranks assets after rights fee. The strongest renewal logic sits with high-quality inventory that combines strong placement, audience quality, engagement, and a plausible outcome link. Assets with broad reach but weak counterfactual evidence should be negotiated around data access, holdouts, and performance clauses rather than accepted at the current fee.

| Decision lane | Operating recommendation | Rationale | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renew / scale | Jersey front; strategic timeout; fan park | High modeled quality score and positive value after fee. | Medium |
| Renegotiate | Boundary LED; digital match hub; creator matchday | Positive commercial case, but value depends on evidence quality and fee discipline. | Low to medium |
| Test before renewal | Retail take-over | Attractive conversion path, but retail lift needs clean sell-out and control-market data. | Medium |
| Exit or replace if uninstrumented | Training ground | Smaller modeled value and weaker proof of downstream impact. | Low |

### Analytical approach

The model uses a transparent quality-adjusted contact score. It combines visibility duration, screen placement, audience quality, engagement quality, and conversion quality. The output includes a direct-conversion case, an assisted-conversion plus retail-lift case, and a conservative control-market lift case.

The portfolio uncertainty view reports a modeled ROI range of **48.7% at P10, 66.9% at P50, and 84.0% at P90**. These are deterministic-seed perturbation ranges around simulated lift assumptions. They are not real-world confidence intervals. A live decision should replace them with bootstrap intervals from logged treatment and control observations.

### Commercial negotiation position

The renewal conversation should anchor on three asks. First, require an asset-level exposure log with placement, duration, legibility, audience, and frequency fields. Second, require access to consented, de-identified outcome measurement through a clean-room or equivalent process. Third, convert a portion of the rights fee into a performance tranche that depends on pre-agreed measurement quality and incremental lift.

The simulator uses **50% ROI as the scale bar, 15% ROI as the renegotiation bar, and 0% ROI as the test/exit boundary**. These are proposed decision rules, not market benchmarks.

### Recommendation

Proceed with a renewal architecture rather than a blanket renewal. Scale only the inventory that clears the quality and incrementality bar. Preserve a measured test budget for retail and digital activation. Exit or replace assets whose economics remain dependent on raw reach after the next measurement sprint.

### What management should approve now

Approve the data contract, treatment-control design, and rights negotiation clauses before the next season. Do not approve incremental spend solely on public reach, social interactions, or media-equivalent value.
